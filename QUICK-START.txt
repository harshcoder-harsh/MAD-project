   QUICK START - COPY THESE COMMANDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In your terminal, run these commands ONE BY ONE:

1. source ~/.nvm/nvm.sh

2. nvm use 20

3. npx expo start

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After running "npx expo start":
- Wait 10-15 seconds
- A QR code will appear
- Open Expo Go app on your Android phone
- Scan the QR code
- Your app will load!
